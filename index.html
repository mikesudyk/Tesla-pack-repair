Fault Summary

18 of 22 codes cleared with Toolbox 3. 4 remaining:

  • BMS_f107 — Pyro fuse open (or sense issue)
  • BMS_w107 — Pyro fuse warning
  • BMS_a129 — Cell imbalance
  • BMS_a130 — Cell imbalance

Root Cause

Pyro fuse cover gasket failure (cover P/N 1020227-00-C) allowed water into the cavity. This is the source of the f107/w107 and likely contributed to sense-wire issues.

Connector Issue vs Real Cell Imbalance

SMT high spread with TBMM low spread = BMB/connector or sense-wire problem, not cell degradation.

Module 12 example: SMT 81 mV → TBMM 4 mV PASS.

Sense Wire Repair Guide (Riveting Method)

For detached sense wires (e.g. Module 16 orange/green):

  1. Clean the tab and wire end.
  2. Use a small rivet or crimp to reattach.
  3. Confirm continuity and retest with TBMM.

How to choose the balance point

After repair, balance to the lowest healthy cell group or the pack average, then recheck.
