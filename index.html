Pyro Fuse / Water Ingress

Water confirmed in pyro fuse cavity. Cover P/N 1020227-00-C (gasket failure) is the source of the f107/w107 and likely contributed to sense-wire issues.

Pyro fuse TPN 1053124-00-A / TSN T15E0022094.

Why SMT Spreads Were High

Most high SMT spreads (e.g. Module 12 at 81 mV, Module 16 at 150 mV) were caused by detached or high-resistance sense wires, not actual cell imbalance. TBMM (with good connections) showed true low imbalance (3–5 mV) after riveting the loose wires.

Sense Wire Repair Guide (Riveting Method)

For detached sense wires (e.g. Module 16 orange/green, Module 9 green/brown/orange):

  1. Clean the tab and wire end.
  2. Use a small rivet or crimp to reattach.
  3. Tug-test the connection.
  4. Retest with TBMM.

Colored boxes in the inspection table match the physical wire colors (top: red/yellow/blue/black; bottom: orange/green/brown).
